Straight answers

Good to know.

Anything else, ring Kurt on 0400 850 354.

Do you do house demolition?+

Yes. Full house demolitions, knockdown and rebuild projects, residential site clearing and preparation for construction.

Can you knock down just part of the house?+

Yes. Partial demolition takes out only the section that needs to go: rear additions, garages, carports, verandahs, internal walls and renovation prep. The rest stays standing.

Do you remove asbestos?+

Yes, for homes and commercial sites, including before a knockdown. Read more about asbestos removal.

Do you do shop, factory and office strip outs?+

Yes. Fixtures, fittings, partitions, ceilings, flooring, workstations, machinery and anything else that has to go, for a refit, redevelopment or lease handover.

Do you handle insurance jobs?+

Yes. Fire, storm and water damage demolition works.

How do I get a quote?+

Call or text Kurt on 0400 850 354, or Brandon on 0434 984 458. We come out and quote in person.
