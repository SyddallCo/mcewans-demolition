Asbestos removal in Melbourne

Why it comes out before anything comes down

Plenty of Melbourne homes built before 1990 still have asbestos somewhere in them: the eaves, the wet areas, the floors or the old shed out the back. Disturbing it is the problem, which is why it has to come out safely before a knockdown, a partial demolition or a renovation strip out starts.

McEwan’s does asbestos removal and demolition, so a knockdown and rebuild can be one booking with one crew instead of two trades and two quotes.

Know what you’re dealing with

Where asbestos hides in older homes

  1. 1
    Roof sheetsCorrugated fibro on houses, garages and sheds
  2. 2
    EavesFlat fibro lining under the roof line
  3. 3
    WallsFlat or patterned fibro sheeting, inside and out
  4. 4
    Bathroom and laundryWall linings behind tiles, splashbacks
  5. 5
    FloorsOld vinyl tiles and the backing under sheet vinyl
  6. 6
    Sheds, fences and fluesFibro fences, old sheds, flues and downpipes
Can’t tell? Most people can’t by looking. If the place was built before 1990, treat it as possible until it’s checked.

Permits and rules

The rules in Victoria

WorkSafe Victoria

Asbestos removal in Victoria is regulated by WorkSafe. What your job needs depends on the type of asbestos and how much there is.

EPA Victoria

Asbestos waste can only go to a site licensed by EPA Victoria to accept it.

Asbestos removal questions

Do you do asbestos removal in Melbourne?+

Yes, for homes and commercial sites right across Melbourne, from our base in Narre Warren.

Can you remove asbestos before knocking the house down?+

Yes. We do asbestos removal and demolition, so it can be one booking: asbestos out first, then the knockdown.

Where is asbestos usually found in a home?+

Common spots are fibro walls and eaves, wet area wall linings, old vinyl floor tiles and backing, fibro roof sheets, fences, flues and sheds.

How do I know if my house has asbestos?+

You usually can’t tell by looking. If the home was built before 1990, treat it as possible until it’s checked. We’ll look when we come out to quote.

Do I need a licensed removalist?+

Asbestos removal in Victoria is regulated by WorkSafe Victoria, and what’s needed depends on the type and amount. We’ll tell you exactly what your job needs when we quote.

What happens to the asbestos waste?+

It’s taken off site to a facility licensed by EPA Victoria to accept asbestos waste.

How much does asbestos removal cost?+
